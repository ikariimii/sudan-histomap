## 1. Mamluk southern limit on the Nile (PRIORITY)
- 1265: Mamluks invade northern Makuria, king forced into vassalage [WP_MAMLUK, citing Behrens-Abouseif 2014, no page].
- 20 Jan 1276: Baybars' army takes Gebel Adda, Meinarti, defeats the Makurians at Dongola, sacks it. 4 Jun 1276 Mashkouda installed, swears fealty: Makuria becomes a **Mamluk vassal**. Tribute + baqt imposed; **transfer of Lower Nubia and jizya "probably never put into action"** [WP_MAKURIA_2026 ← Seignobos 2018 pp.139–143].
- 1316: Mamluk-installed Abdallah Barshanbu (first Muslim king). Late 1317: Kanz al-Dawla (Banu Kanz emir from Aswan) kills him. 1323: al-Nasir Muhammad's army installs Kudanbes; after it leaves Kanz al-Dawla retakes the throne and the Mamluks recognise him **in return for tribute** [WP_MAKURIA_2026 ← Seignobos 2020 pp.146–151].
- 1365: Makurian civil war, rump state at Gebel Adda (Lower Nubia), last king c.1460 [WP_MAKURIA_2026].
- Qasr Ibrim: Ayyubid capture 1173 (pre-Mamluk) [WP_MAKURIA_2026 ← Werner 2013 p.113]. No source found for a Mamluk garrison there; unsourced.
- **Recommendation:** directly ruled limit = **Aswan / First Cataract (~24.09°N)**, 1250–1517, confidence medium-high. Hatch Makuria 1276–c.1365 as "Mamluk tributary/vassal (client)" — not in the Mamluk polygon. Red Sea: Suakin / Dahlak said "conquered" c.1265 [WP_MAMLUK ← Nicolle 2014] — treat as point/badge, low confidence.
- Pass 19b: HD excerpt (hd.pdf, 23 pp.) has no Mamluk material; GHA V file is invalid. **Mamluk answer stays Wikipedia-only (flagged).**
- Unsourced: a scholarly (non-WP) page cite for Aswan as the formal border; check Holt & Daly (HD2011) or Welsby 2002 ch. on Mamluks.

## 2. New Kingdom Egypt
- South: Thutmose I (c.1530–1520 BCE per GHA) set his inscription at Tumbus (3rd Cataract) and occupied "the whole reach between Kerma and **Kurgus**, 50 miles south of Abu Hamed" [GHA2_CH10_P265, p.265]. Hagar el-Merwa at Kurgus carries boundary inscriptions of Thutmose I and Thutmose III, "southern border on the Nile" [WP_KANISAH_KURGUS ← Török 2009 p.104]. Administration: Wawat (Nekhen–2nd Cat.) and Kush (2nd–4th Cat.) under the viceroy [GHA2 p.270].
- Napata / Gebel Barkal (18.53N 31.84E [WP_NAPATA]) lies inside that reach. Recommend: southern limit = **Kurgus** (boundary marker), settled zone to **4th Cataract/Napata**; from c.1525 BCE to end of New Kingdom (GHA: −1580 to −1050). Confidence: medium. Kurgus coordinates/Wikidata QID NOT verified — Map Designer must look up (do not guess).
- Levant: Thutmose III crossed the Euphrates against Mitanni [WP_THUTMOSE3] → max reach to Euphrates (Carchemish area), low confidence as control. After Kadesh (May 1274 BCE, Orontes near Lake Homs, inconclusive) [WP_KADESH] use the Kadesh/Amurru line. Treaty date (1259 BCE) not verified in my grep — unsourced. Wikidata IDs for Carchemish/Kadesh: not looked up.

## 3. Wadai, Bornu, Zande
- **Wadai:** emerges 1630s replacing Tunjur; tributary of Darfur in 17th c. until Ya'qub Arus (late 17th c.); 1805 conquers Bagirmi (tributary); Sabun extends south to Dar Sila and Dar Runga; borders Darfur and Bagirmi [WP_WADAI]. No polygon source. Recommend points + labelled neighbours, low.
- **Bornu:** Dunama II (13th c.) rule to Fezzan; eastern limit unclear, later tradition says to Nile, contemporary evidence only "influence" in Darfur [WP_KANEM_BORNU ← Barth 1857 p.637; Waziri 2023 p.9]. Do NOT draw to the Nile. Capitals: Ngazargamu c.1472–1809, none 1809–13, Kafela 1813–46, Kukawa 1846–93, Dikwa 1893–1902.
- **Zande:** Avongara-led chiefdoms spreading east and north from SE CAR; northward expansion halted by Sudanese raids in 19th c. [WP_AZANDE]. Nothing with dates/limits: keep note only.
- **Pass 19b GHA VI cites:**
  - Wadai: 'grown increasingly powerful after the mid-eighteenth century'; Benghazi–Wadai route discovered early 19th c. [GHA6_SAHARA_P530]. 1846 Wadai invaded Borno at mai Ibrahim's invitation [GHA6_BORNU_P594_598, p.594]. Borno's expansion was blocked 'in the east by Baghirmi and Wadai' [p.598] → Borno's eastern limit = Baghirmi/Wadai (19th c.), medium.
  - Zande: Avungara 'founded a centralized state in the eighteenth century' south of the Jieng/Nuer lands; Ndoruma defeated Abu Kurun 1870 [GHA6_CH14_P368, p.368]. Core area: Mbomu valley south to the Uele, high density in the 19th c. [GHA6_CENTRAL_P300, p.300]. → Zande anchor zone Mbomu–Uele, 18th c.–1880s, low-medium; no line limits.
  - Still unsourced in GHA: precise Wadai western/southern lines, Bornu 13th-c. extent, Ngazargamu dates (GHA V needed).

## 4. Ethiopia (Solomonic) phases [WP_ETHIOPIAN_EMPIRE]
1270 Yekuno Amlak; 1314–1344 Amda Seyon conquests (Gojjam, Damot, Eritrea, Muslim east); 1529–1543 Adal war (Ahmad Gragn); 1557–1589 Ottoman war, loss of Red Sea coast; 1636 Gondar; 1769–1855 Zemene Mesafint (decentralised); 1855 Tewodros; Menelik expansion later. No shapes sourced — phase notes/points only, low.
- **Pass 19b GHA VI ch.15 (Pankhurst):** in the Zamana Masafent the emperors were 'mere puppets'; Bagemder was 'site of the capital' and Gondar 'the only urban agglomeration of any size' (p.376); Amhara's significance came from 'its control of the imperial capital, Gondar' (p.379); Shoa under Asfa Wassan (1775–1808) 'severed the last links of vassalage to Gondar' (p.381) → Shoa outside Gondar's control c.1800–1855; Kassa burnt Dabra Tabor 1853, closing the Masafent, captured Beru 1854, beat Webé at Darasgé, then crowned Téwodros (p.391; Plowden 1855); after coronation he took Magdala, 'which was to become his capital', and overran Shoa (p.393). Phases 1270–1769 remain Wikipedia-only (GHA IV/V not usable).

## 5. Capital end years
- **Ngazargamu: 1809** (destroyed by Fulani 1808, briefly reoccupied, abandoned 1809 by Dunama IX) [WP_KANEM_BORNU ← Stapleton 2013 p.81; Brenner 1973 pp.33–34]; WP_NGAZARGAMU "abandoned 1808/1809". Confidence high.
- **Gondar: 1855** as end — now GHA-backed: Téwodros crowned after Darasgé (1855) and made Magdala his capital [GHA6_CH15_P376_393, pp.391, 393]. Original WP basis: (Tewodros crowned 1855, ending Zemene Mesafint; he took Gondar 1854 but "was not attracted to Gondar") [WP_ETHIOPIAN_EMPIRE; WP_GONDAR]. Note: from 1769 Gondar was the seat of powerless emperors. Confidence medium.

## 6. Aksum & South Arabia
- GHA II: Kaleb's expedition against Dhu Nuwas c.525; garrison of 10,000 left; Sumyafa Ashwa installed at Zafar under Abreha [GHA2_CH16 p.415]; Abraha as vassal king of Himyar paying yearly tribute to Aksum, Aksum appointing south-Arabian kings [GHA2 pp.385–386].
- Abraha reign 535–560/570, d. c.570; Persian intervention 570–578 [WP_ABRAHA; WP_AKSUM].
- **Recommendation:** include Yemen/Himyar only **525–c.570 as Aksumite vassal (Himyar under Sumyafa Ashwa then Abraha)**; whether Abraha stayed loyal after usurping (c.535) is not verified here, so badge 535–570 "tributary, contested"; exclude before 525 and after 570. A pre-632 shape including Arabia throughout is wrong. Confidence medium. (A 3rd-c. Aksumite presence in Tihama exists in the literature but was not verified here — unsourced.)

### (a) Mamluks & Makuria — now GHA-backed (supersedes WP-only)
- Frontier: Fatimids stationed troops "at Aswan to guard the frontier" after the 1102–3 Banu Kanz revolt [GHA4_CH16, p.401].
- 1276: Baybars' expedition defeated Dawud, enthroned Shakanda; oath "would practically have replaced the traditional bakt relationship by complete vassalage": half the revenue as tribute, jizya, and "Maris, or more probably its revenue, was assigned to the direct control of the sultan" [p.403]. Mamluk garrison left at Dunkula, killed by Shamamun; 1290 Qalawun accepts augmented bakt [p.403]. 1305 expedition [p.403].
- 1317: Barshambu (first Muslim king), mosque tablet 29 May 1317; killed by Kanz al-Dawla Shuja' al-Din; 1323–4 expedition restores Karanbas, Kanz al-Dawla drives him out, no further Mamluk intervention [p.404]. 1365–6 Mamluk troops sent at Nubian request; kings at al-Daw; last reference 1397 [p.404].
- **"The recorded facts indicate that Nubia was never annexed"** [p.405].
- Early 15th c.: "probable loss of the devastated province of Aswan" from Mamluk control [GHA4_EGYPT_P392].
- **Recommendation (now medium-high, GHA-sourced):** Mamluk polygon south limit = Aswan / First Cataract 1250–1517; Makuria 1276–c.1365 hatched "vassal/tributary"; optional 1276 badge "Maris (Lower Nubia) – revenue assigned to sultan, control uncertain". Badge Aswan itself "contested/lost" c.1400–1420s (date range approximate; source gives only "probable").

### (b) Ngazargamu end
- Founded c.1472 by Ali Gaji, capital "throughout [Sefuwa] rule in Borno" [GHA5_BORNU, p.493]. GHA VI p.559: reformers captured it, couldn't hold it, "the site was simply abandoned" — **no year in GHA**. Keep **1809** (1808 sack / 1809 abandonment) from WP_KANEM_BORNU (Stapleton 2013; Brenner 1973), flagged; confidence medium-high.

### (c) Bornu extent & Wadai limits
- Kanem zenith tied to Fezzan–Kawar route [GHA4_KANEM_P247] — no line limits in GHA; 13th-c. extent still WP-only, do NOT extend to Nile.
- Borno 16th–17th c.: metropolitan province ~20 000 km² [p.498]; satellites Mandara and Bagirmi [p.497]; Wadai founded by Abd al-Karim (r.1611–55) inside Borno's orbit, checking Bagirmi [pp.511–512]; Bagirmi independent under Muhammad al-Amin (c.1715–85), Wadai under Jawda (1747–95) expanding toward Bahr al-Ghazal (Chad) [p.513] → Borno eastern limit: Bagirmi until c.1715, then Borno proper (~Lake Chad/Kanem).
- Wadai–Darfur: Wadai west of Darfur with semi-independent buffer kingdoms between; Dar Zaghawa contested, "resolved in favour of the Kayra" [GHA5_CH7, pp.187–188]. Recommend Wadai east limit = buffer zone (badge), low-medium. No GHA coordinates.

### (d) Ethiopia 1270–1769 — now GHA-backed
- 1270–1314: territories "limited to the old Zagwe possessions, with some unimportant recent acquisitions in the Shoa area" [GHA4_CH17, p.434].
- 1314–44 Amde-Siyon: Damot & Hadya 1316–17, then Gojjam, Ifat c.1332; tribute from dependent territories [pp.434–435, 438].
- 1529 Sembera Kure → Muslim armies over the highlands until 1543 [GHA5_CH24, p.713]; Lebna Dengel d.1540, Galawdewos restoration [p.714]; 1529–1632 "struggle for survival" [p.723].
- 1636 Fasiladas' fixed residence at Gondar [p.725]; disintegration/anarchy after c.1755, to 1855 [pp.724, 733].
- Still unsourced: polygons for any phase; WP Zemene Mesafint start 1769 vs GHA "after about 1755" — use 1755 (GHA) with 1769 noted.
