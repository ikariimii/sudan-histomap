Kingdom of Taqali

1750 CE – 1969 CE

Dating note

Founding c.1750 and end 1969 NOT verified. Holt & Daly: semi-autonomous after 1821 and integrated into Condominium local government; royal line claims Ja'ali holy-man ancestry.

Key events

  • Nuba Mountains kingdom; tributary to Funj/Darfur at times
  • Incorporated into Condominium administration

Capitals / sites

Tegali / Tasi

Geopolitics

Lay in the north-eastern foothills of the Nuba Mountains. Its rulers 'encouraged the immigration of settlers and established their suzerainty over a considerable area'. It survived in semi-autonomy after the Turco-Egyptian conquest and was integrated into Condominium local government (HD2011, intro p.7). Takana places the rise of Tagali in the 17th century, alongside the Fur and Lake Chad sultanates (p.129), and names Sinnar and Tagali as Darfur's eastern neighbours (p.173).

Culture

A small Muslim state in the Nuba Mountains whose royal family 'derives its origin from the marriage of a Ja'ali holy man with an indigenous princess' (HD2011, intro p.4).
