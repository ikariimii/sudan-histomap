Dating note

Start corrected 1603→c.1650 (O'Fahey 2008). Older chronologies place Sulayman earlier; founding date genuinely uncertain. El Fasher date c.1790–91.

Key events

  • Keira dynasty from Jabal Marra; founder Sulayman (Solongdungu) – O'Fahey dates the sultanate c.1650–1916
  • Expansion into Kordofan under Muhammad Tayrab c.1785
  • Capital fixed at al-Fashir c.1790/91 under 'Abd al-Rahman
  • Conquered by al-Zubayr Rahma 1874

Capitals / sites

El Fasher

Geopolitics

Wars with Wadai in the west; eastward expansion into Kordofan c.1785; Kordofan lost after 1821; conquered by al-Zubayr 1874 (O'Fahey 2008 ToC; Holt & Daly).

Culture

Muslim Fur dynasty claiming 'Abbasi ancestry (Holt & Daly).

Economy

The Forty Days' Road (Darb al-arba'in) from Kubayh to Asyut in Egypt; slave raiding and trading (Holt & Daly; O'Fahey ToC).
