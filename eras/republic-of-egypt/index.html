Geopolitics

Egypt is Sudan's most important northern neighbour and a party to the Nile regime. The 8 Nov 1959 Cairo agreement allowed Egypt to build the Sudd el-Aali (High Dam) at Aswan and Sudan the Roseires reservoir (UNTS6519_1959). From 1 Feb 1958 Egypt claimed the territory north of the 22nd parallel, opening the Halaib dispute (IBS18_1962) [CONTESTED]; effective Egyptian control dates are contested – 1992/93, 1994, 1995 or 2000 depending on source (see db/halaib_dates_check.md; WP_HALAIB). Under Nimeiri, Sudan backed Egypt after the 1979 peace with Israel; Mubarak gave Nimeiri asylum in 1985, and after 1989 relations collapsed: Egypt hosted the opposition NDA, accused Khartoum of aiming Iraqi missiles at the High Dam, and by mid-1991 openly funded the DUP and SPLM (LOC1991_EGYPT; LOC1991_FOREIGN). Egypt has always opposed the GERD; Sudan, after first backing it under Bashir, hardened its stance after the Oct 2021 coup as the two armies held joint exercises (GEO-015). In the 2023 war, Egypt and Saudi Arabia back the Sudanese army (ICG_ETH_ERI) [VERIFY]; see also GEO-012 (Egyptian state-affiliated, pro-SAF) and AR_AJCS_5653.

Culture

Egypt's main cultural presence in independent Sudan noted in the sources read is higher education: around 1990 the Khartoum branch of the University of Cairo, with 13,000 students, was 'larger but less prestigious' than the University of Khartoum, and it was the only university where tuition was free (LOC1991_EDUCATION). Earlier, Azhari had been 'the major spokesman for the ‘unity of the Nile Valley’' before reversing the NUP's stand and supporting independence, declared 19 December 1955 (LOC1991_INDSUDAN). [SINGLE-SOURCE per point; dated 1991]

Economy

Water is the core economic tie. The 1959 agreement set 'established rights' of 48 bn m³/yr for Egypt and 4 bn m³/yr for Sudan (at Aswan); the High Dam's net benefit of 22 bn m³ (mean flow 84 bn m³ minus 10 bn m³ storage losses) was split 14.5 to Sudan and 7.5 to Egypt, giving totals of 55.5 bn m³ (Egypt) and 18.5 bn m³ (Sudan) (UNTS6519_1959) [VERIFY against full PDF]. Chatham House notes Sudan 'currently takes far less' than its allocated share (GEO-015) [SINGLE-SOURCE]. A 1990 Libya–Sudan rapprochement was 'not viewed favorably in Cairo' (LOC1991_LIBYA). The event file's Aswan High Dam/Wadi Halfa flooding item belongs to this agreement.
