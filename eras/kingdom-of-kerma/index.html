Origins & Kerma · Sudan – Middle Nile

Kingdom of Kerma

2450 BCE – 1480 BCE

Approximate area of control No data

No extent file for this era yet, so nothing is drawn.

Dating note

Corrected to Swiss Archaeological Mission chronology (kerma.ch). Note GHA II (1981) still dates 'Kerma' to -1730/-1580, i.e. only the Classic phase; superseded by Bonnet/Honegger excavations.

Key events

  • Western Deffufa mud-brick temple
  • Largest African state outside Egypt in the 2nd millennium BCE
  • Sacked by Thutmose I c.1500 BCE
  • Swiss mission phases: Early Kerma c.2450–2050, Middle c.2050–1750, Classic c.1750–1480 BCE (Final Kerma to c.1450)

Capitals / sites

Kerma

Geopolitics

Rival of Egypt; the Middle Kingdom fixed its southern frontier at Semna; Kerma was sacked by Thutmose I c.1500 BCE.

Culture

Indigenous Nubian urban culture: deffufa temples and royal tumuli in the Classic phase (kerma.ch).
