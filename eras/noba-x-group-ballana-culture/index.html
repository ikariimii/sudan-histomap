Christian Nubia · Sudan – Middle Nile

Noba / X-Group (Ballana culture)

350 CE – 550 CE

Dating note

Approximate. Ballana coordinates verified: Pleiades 795783 (Barrington Atlas 81 B3). Site now under Lake Nasser.

Key events

  • Post-Meroitic royal tumuli at Ballana and Qustul

Capitals / sites

Ballana

Geopolitics

Post-Meroitic struggle between the Blemmyes and the Nobades; the Nobades won control of the Nile valley and pushed the Blemmyes into the eastern desert; Leclant inclines to see the Nobades as the lords of Ballana and Qustul (GHA II ch.10, 12).

Culture

Rich royal tumuli at Ballana (excavated by Emery, 1938); some Christian presence before the official conversion is argued from Faras (dating contested) (GHA II ch.12).
