Dating note

Meroitic chronology uncertain (GHA II: 'particularly serious' for 2nd–1st c. BCE). Which kandake negotiated with Rome (Amanirenas vs Amanishakheto) is unresolved in GHA. End placed in the 4th century CE (350 is a placeholder year); cause debated: GHA II ascribes the overthrow to the Noba and concludes the kingdom 'had already collapsed at the time of Ezana's campaign'.

Key events

  • Ergamenes/Arkamani (3rd c. BCE), friendly relations with Ptolemaic Egypt
  • Queen Shanakdakhete (c.170–160 BCE): among earliest Meroitic hieroglyphic inscriptions (Naqa)
  • Taniydamani (late 2nd c. BCE): first long Meroitic texts
  • Iron working at Meroe; Meroitic script transliterated by Griffith 1909, language still not understood
  • Kandakes Amanirenas and Amanishakheto; Prince/King Akinidad; war with Rome: Napata taken by Petronius 23 BCE (GHA), Treaty of Samos 21/20 BCE
  • Natakamani & Amanitore (GHA: c.12 BCE–12 CE; dating debated): Naqa Lion Temple, Amara, restoration of Napata
  • Collapsed in the 4th century CE; cause debated (Noba per GHA II; Aksumite campaign of Ezana)

Capitals / sites

Meroë, Naqa, Musawwarat es-Sufra, Wad ban Naqa, Hamadab, Sedeinga

Geopolitics

After Petronius took Napata (23 BCE), the Treaty of Samos (21/20 BCE) renounced Roman tribute and set the frontier at Hiera Sycaminos (GHA II). Naga is described as Meroe's southernmost city and a 'powerful rival' of Ptolemaic and Roman Egypt (Naga project). Collapse by the time of Ezana's campaign, c. mid-4th c. CE.

Culture

Local innovations: Meroitic writing, official cult of indigenous gods such as the lion-god Apedemak, ruling queens (Kandakes) (French national-museum text). Naga combines African, Egyptian and Hellenistic elements (Naga project).

Economy

Iron working at Meroe (large slag heaps, WHC/GHA); Red Sea trade with Greek merchants (PRC Embassy text); great hafir reservoirs (DAI Musawwarat).

Regional power

  • Meroe/Kush: Research in progress sources describe Meroe as a 'powerful rival' of Egypt (Naga project) but give no measure
