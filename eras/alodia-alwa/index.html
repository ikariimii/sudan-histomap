Christian Nubia · Sudan – Middle Nile

Alodia (Alwa)

500 CE – 1504 CE

Approximate area of control No data

No extent file for this era yet, so nothing is drawn.

Dating note

Fall c.1504 traditional (Funj/'Abdallab). Soba point confirmed by Wikidata Q1247214 (15.524N 32.681E), consistent with the original; there is no Pleiades entry; no excavation-report coordinate checked.

Key events

  • Christian kingdom on the Blue/White Nile
  • Capital Soba
  • Fell to Funj/Abdallab c.1504

Capitals / sites

Soba

Geopolitics

Southernmost of the three Christian Nubian kingdoms by the end of the 6th c. (Nobadia, Makuria, Alodia) (GHA II ch.12).

Culture

A Byzantine mission under Bishop Longinos reached Alodia c.580 with Nobadian support and found it already partly converted by the Aksumites (GHA II ch.12).
