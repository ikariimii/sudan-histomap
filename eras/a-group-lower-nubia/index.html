Origins & Kerma · Sudan – Middle Nile

A-Group (Lower Nubia)

3800 BCE – 2900 BCE

Approximate area of control No data

No extent file for this era yet, so nothing is drawn.

Dating note

UNESCO GHA II ch.9 (Sherif) places the A-Group's flowering c.3100 BCE, contemporary with Egypt's 1st Dynasty; -3800 start is a broad conventional bracket. Qustul coordinates corrected (Wikidata).

Key events

  • Trade with Predynastic/Early Dynastic Egypt
  • Qustul elite cemetery

Capitals / sites

Qustul, Faras

Geopolitics

Flourished c.3100 BCE, contemporary with Egypt's First Dynasty; remains found between the First Cataract and Batn el-Hagar (GHA II ch.9).

Culture

Non-literate culture known only archaeologically; fine thin pottery; A-Group-type pottery also found farther south (e.g. near Omdurman) (GHA II ch.9).

Economy

Copper tools (earliest metal tools found in Sudan) and imported Egyptian pottery; a period of prosperity and population growth (GHA II ch.9).
