Geopolitics

The National Unionist Party (successor of the pro-Egyptian Ashikka') won the November 1953 elections but steered toward full independence. Parliament declared independence on 19 Dec 1955, proclaimed on 1 Jan 1956. The Equatoria Corps mutiny of August 1955 began the southern problem (GHA VIII pp.148–150).

Culture

The two largest religious orders, the Ansar and the Khatmiyya, agreed in Dec 1953 to work for independence (GHA VIII).

Economy

The Gezira cotton scheme, run since 1950 by the government's Sudan Gezira Board, was 'the backbone of the Sudan economy' and involved about 26,000 Sudanese tenant farmers in 1958 (GHA VIII p.322).
