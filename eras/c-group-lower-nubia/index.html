C-Group (Lower Nubia)

2400 BCE – 1550 BCE Arabic under review

Approximate area of control

No sourced map of this era's territory yet, so no area is drawn.

Dating note

GHA II ch.9: C-Group appears late Old Kingdom/First Intermediate Period and lasts until Egyptianisation of Nubia in the 16th c. BCE; -2400 start sits at the early edge. Aniba verified: Pleiades 795777 (BAtlas 81 C3).

Key events

  • Pastoralist culture contemporary with Kerma

Capitals / sites

Aniba

Geopolitics

Appeared in Lower Nubia late in the Old Kingdom or First Intermediate Period; lasted until the full Egyptianisation of Nubia in the 16th c. BCE; northern limit at Kubanieh North (GHA II ch.9).

Culture

Chalcolithic culture with its own burial traditions; brick chapels against tombs; continued despite increasing contact with Egypt (GHA II ch.9).

Economy

'Essentially cattle-herding people who lived in small camps or occasionally settled in villages'; inferred from rock pictures of cattle and the prominence of cattle in burial rites (GHA II ch.9, p.252).
