Ja'aliyyin kingdom (Sa'dab, Funj vassal)

1500 CE – 1821 CE Arabic under review

Approximate area of control

Research in progress No sourced map of this era's territory yet, so no area is drawn.

Dating note

'From the sixteenth century' (Holt & Daly); 1500 approximate.

Key events

  • Tribal kingdom between the Atbara confluence and the Sabaluqa Gorge from the 16th c.
  • Royal clan the Sa'dab; Mirafab kingdom at Berber to the north
  • Shendi became the chief commercial centre of the eastern Bilad al-Sudan by early 19th c., under Makk Nimr

Capitals / sites

Shendi

Geopolitics

Tribal kingdom of the Sa'dab between the Atbara confluence and the Sabaluqa Gorge; Shendi under Makk Nimr was autonomous before 1821 (Holt & Daly).

Culture

The Ja'ali Group claim descent from an Arab ancestor, Ibrahim Ja'al, later made a descendant of al-'Abbas, so 'Ja'ali' and ''Abbasi' became virtual synonyms; Holt & Daly see a 'submerged Nubian substratum' as their common element, with Arab ancestry from intermarriage and a Muslim cultural inheritance (HD2011, intro p.3).

Economy

Shendi, where the two trade axes met, was the main commercial centre of the eastern Bilad al-Sudan by the early 19th century (Holt & Daly).
