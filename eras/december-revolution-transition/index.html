Geopolitics

Bashir deposed by the military on 11 Apr 2019 after the most sustained civilian protest movement in Sudan's modern history; security forces' attack on protesters on 3 June 2019; 17 Aug 2019 power-sharing deal with an 11-member Sovereign Council; Hamdok PM; UNITAMS created 2020; 25 Oct 2021 coup dissolved the transitional government (ICG; UNSC 2524).

Culture

Murals and graffiti, mostly on walls outside army HQ where protesters camped, became with music a symbol of the uprising; protest leaders (FFC) protested their whitewashing in Aug 2019 (AFP_GRAFFITI2019, 2019-08-14). Murals carried the three slogans حرية سلام وعدالة ('freedom, peace, justice') (AYIN_MURALS2022, 2022-03-12; the slogan is also visible on an EPA photo caption of 5 Jul 2019 in AFP_GRAFFITI2019). Erasure began with the 3 Jun 2019 sit-in break-up and resumed after the Oct 2021 coup; one artist estimates 70% of revolution murals removed [SINGLE-SOURCE, one interviewee: AYIN_MURALS2022]. Women: said to be up to 70% of protesters, though JPIA notes 'the precise source of this number is unclear' [SINGLE-SOURCE, hedged: JPIA_GENDER2020 §2, 2020-05-01]. Symbols included the white toub, the 'Kandaka' title (from Kushite queens; some activists criticised it) and the viral Apr 2019 image of Alaa Salah, whose framing as a lone icon Sudanese activists contested (JPIA_GENDER2020 §3.1–3.3). The transition repealed most public order laws and criminalised FGM, and the Constitutional Declaration set a 40% women's quota for a legislature that had not been formed by Apr 2020 (JPIA_GENDER2020 §5.1–5.2).

Economy

The transitional government ran an IMF Staff-Monitored Program from 2020 (IMF_CR21082, 2021-03-08). On 21 Feb 2021 the Central Bank unified the official and parallel rates under a 'managed float' (AJA_FLOAT2021, 2021-02-21; IMF_CR21143 para 6, 2021-06-21); bankers put the indicative rate at SDG 375/USD against the old official 55 [SINGLE-SOURCE: AJA_FLOAT2021 citing Reuters]. The IMF lists fuel-subsidy removal, higher electricity tariffs and 2021 budget tax measures alongside unification (IMF_CR21082). Inflation: 304.33% y-o-y in Jan 2021 [SINGLE-SOURCE: AJA_FLOAT2021 citing Anadolu] and 342% y-o-y in Mar 2021 [SINGLE-SOURCE: IMF_CR21143 para 6], driven by fuel price hikes and food/fuel shortages. On 29 Jun 2021 Sudan reached the HIPC Decision Point (38th country): external debt c. USD 56bn NPV (163% of GDP, end-2020), with total relief of more than USD 50bn NPV, over 90% of external debt, if Completion Point were reached in about three years; enhanced-HIPC relief USD 23.3bn [SINGLE-SOURCE: IMF_PR21199, a joint IMF/WB release, 2021-06-29]. After the 25 Oct 2021 coup the World Bank paused disbursements in all Sudan operations (WB_PAUSE2021, 2021-10-27). Earlier context: ICG2019 describes a 'crushing economic crisis'.
