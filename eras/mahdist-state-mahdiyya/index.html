Mahdist State (Mahdiyya)

1885 CE – 1899 CE

Capitals / sites

Omdurman

Geopolitics

A jihad movement that defeated Hicks at Shaykan (5 Nov 1883), took Kordofan, Darfur and Bahr al-Ghazal, and with 'Uthman Digna the east except Suakin; Khartoum fell and Gordon was killed (26 Jan 1885). Under the Khalifa: defeat at Tushki (1889), loss of Tokar (1891) and Kassala (1894), and a long war with Ethiopia that weakened both states. Kitchener's conquest: Atbara (8 Apr 1898), Karari/Omdurman (2 Sep 1898), Umm Diwaykarat (24 Nov 1899) (GHA VII ch.4).

Culture

Aimed to restore the 'pure and primitive faith of Islam'; followers (Ansar) took the bay'a; the hijra to Jabal Qadir shifted leadership to westerners; institutions based on the Qur'an and Sunna; Mahdist sentiment survived the state (GHA VII ch.4).

Economy

Discontent fed by heavy Turkish taxes and by the government's attempts to suppress the slave trade, 'an important source of wealth and the basis of the domestic and agrarian economy' (GHA VII ch.4).
