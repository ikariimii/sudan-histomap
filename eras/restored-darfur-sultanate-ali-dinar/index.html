Geopolitics

'Nominally tributary to, but effectively independent of, the Anglo-Egyptian Sudan'; Ali Dinar answered the Ottoman call to jihad in the First World War, raided French Chad, threatened British Borno and tried to stir revolt in Kordofan; he was defeated and killed in 1916 and Darfur 'fully incorporated into Sudan' (GHA VII ch.12, p.283).
