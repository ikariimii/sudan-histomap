Funj Sultanate of Sennar

1504 CE – 1821 CE

Dating note

1504–1821 per Holt & Daly. Foundation by a Shilluk band is a tradition, not established fact.

Key events

  • Founded by Amara Dunqas 1504
  • Islamisation and Sufi orders
  • Conquered by Ottoman-Egyptian army 1821
  • Holt & Daly: Funj suzerainty over Upper Nubia (above 3rd Cataract) incl. vassal-kingdom of Dongola; 'Abdallab recognised as paramount chiefs of the Arabs

Capitals / sites

Sennar

Geopolitics

Suzerain over Upper Nubia above the Third Cataract, including the vassal kingdom of Dongola; Lower Nubia nominally under Ottoman Egypt. 'Abdallab recognised as paramount chiefs of the Arabs; Kordofan contested with Darfur (Holt & Daly).

Culture

Islamisation and Sufi orders; Arab genealogical identity (Ja'ali/'Abbasi, Juhayna) spreading among riverain peoples (Holt & Daly).

Economy

North–south route Sennar–Egypt and west–east route Darfur–Suakin; Shendi the main market by c.1800; staples millet and dammur cloth used as currency, foreign silver for large deals; slaves of pre-eminent importance in trade (Holt & Daly).
