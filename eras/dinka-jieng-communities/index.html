UNSOURCED START (1500 = display placeholder). No source found giving a datable emergence; Nilotic presence long predates it. Not a state.

Key events

  • Group of tribes without central kingship, mostly in Bahr al-Ghazal floodplains, some east of the White Nile (Holt & Daly)
  • Cattle pastoralism; culturally close to the Nuer, together a Nilotic subdivision (Evans-Pritchard 1940)
