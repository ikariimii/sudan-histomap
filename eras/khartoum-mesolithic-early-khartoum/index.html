Dating note

Corrected -8000/-4900 -> c.8500–5000 cal BC for Early Khartoum ceramics of central Sudan (Garcea/Jesse et al., African Archaeological Review 2021 review). Northern 'Khartoum Variant' is c.8000–4900 cal BC. Site point = city-level approximation.

Key events

  • Wavy-line pottery among the earliest ceramics in Africa
  • Fishing/hunting settlements along the Nile near Khartoum
