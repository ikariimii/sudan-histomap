Capitals / sites

Khartoum

Geopolitics

Declared a joint possession (condominium) of Britain and Egypt in January 1899 and in practice turned into a British colony; Egypt's parliament denounced the 1899 agreement on 15 Oct 1951 (BRE, Orlov).

Culture

Southern Policy developed the South separately: Islamic proselytising banned, Christian missions encouraged, English in mission schools while Arabic became the language of the North (GHA VIII pp.148–150).

Economy

Kitchener's railway reached Khartoum, was linked to the Red Sea in 1905 (Port Sudan and Atbara founded), extended to Sennar and reached Kordofan by 1911; a Sennar–Port Sudan line opened in 1924. Egyptian long-staple cotton; the Gezira Scheme irrigated via the Sennar dam; Gash zone; 1929 Nile waters convention (GHA VIII).
