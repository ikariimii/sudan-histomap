Christian Nubia · Sudan – Middle Nile

'Abdallab chiefdom (Funj vassal)

1480 CE – 1821 CE

Approximate area of control No data

No extent file for this era yet, so nothing is drawn.

Dating note

Start 'late 15th c.' per Holt & Daly; 1480 is approximate. Qarri coordinates from Wikidata (settlement, approximate). End 1821 = Turco-Egyptian conquest.

Key events

  • Founded by 'Abdallah Jamma' (Rufa'a) at the Blue/White Nile confluence, late 15th c.
  • Prospered from tolls on desert nomads
  • Recognised by Funj sultans as paramount chiefs of the Arabs
  • Capital Qarri, later Halfayat al-Muluk; an 'Abdallabi chief ruled Quz Rajab on the Atbara

Capitals / sites

Qarri

Geopolitics

Vassal paramount chiefs under the Funj, centred at Qarri, later Halfayat al-Muluk; controlled Quz Rajab on the Atbara (Holt & Daly).

Culture

Founded in the late 15th century when an Arab population, 'probably of varied origins', settled at the Blue/White Nile junction under 'Abdallah Jamma' of the Rufa'a; the Rufa'a 'preserve some memory of a distinct origin' from the Juhayna (HD2011, intro p.5).

Economy

Wealth from tolls levied on desert nomads during their annual migration (Holt & Daly).
