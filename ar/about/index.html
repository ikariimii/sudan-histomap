Map: Natural Earth 1:50m admin-0 countries (public domain), via world-atlas countries-50m, clipped to the region. Era texts and citations: this project's dataset, sources listed per era in each story card. Area-of-control polygons: db/extents/<era-id>.geojson, drawn only when each feature cites a source; always approximate. Regional capability shares: Correlates of War National Material Capabilities v6.0 (CINC), data ends 2016; editorial scores are labelled EDITORIAL and kept apart.
